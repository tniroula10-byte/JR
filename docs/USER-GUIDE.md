# John Rambo — How to Use It

A short guide for the Boss, employees and customers. Menu names below match what you see in the app.

---

## Signing in

- Go to the site and sign in with your **username or email** and your **password**.
- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in is paused for 15 minutes.
- To get phone notifications: open **Notifications** (the bell) → **Turn on phone notifications**.
  On iPhone, first add the site to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen), then open it from there.

---

## For the Boss

### Add employees
1. **Customers & Invites** → invite form → choose **Employee**.
2. Enter their name, email, a username and a starting password, then **Create Employee & Set Password**.
3. Approve them in the **Pending Customers Queue** on your **Master Dashboard**. Give them the username and password yourself.
4. In **Employee Management**, set their **public alias** (the name customers may see in complaint rooms)
   and what they may do (manage products, publish stories, post announcements).

### Invite customers
1. **Customers & Invites** → enter name and email → **Send Customer Invite**.
2. The customer gets an email with a **6-digit code**. On the sign-in page they tap **Customer OTP Setup**,
   enter the code and choose a password.
3. They then appear in the **Pending Customers Queue** on your Master Dashboard. Tap **Approve** — they get an email and can sign in.

### Set up game top-ups (do this before customers start)
Open **Game Top-ups**:
1. **Games** tab: add the games customers can top up. Up to 11 can be on at once.
2. **Payment tags** tab: add up to 3 payment methods (e.g. Cash App, Venmo, Zelle) and at least one tag for each.
3. **Tag rule** tab, choose one:
   - **Rotate** — everyone sees the same tag; it moves to the next tag after every 5 deposits (you can change 5).
   - **Same tag per customer** — each customer always sees their own tag. You can change a customer's tag at
     the bottom of the Payment tags tab.

### Everyday control
- **Shared Inbox**: every customer chat, with which employee sent each reply (customers only ever see
  "John Rambo Support"). You can reassign a chat to another employee.
- **All Tickets & Assign**: assign new tickets to an employee.
- **Settings**: who sees which chats (all employees, or only assigned ones), ticket auto-close days, and
  whether customers may request a specific employee on a ticket.
- **Staff Analytics**: response times and workload per employee; download as a spreadsheet (CSV).
- **Immutable Audit Log**: every approval, invite, permission change and status change. Nobody can edit it.
- **Suspend** a user from Customers or Employee Management; they are signed out of everything immediately.

## For Employees

### Top-ups (the main job)
1. A new top-up arrives as an **unread chat message** and a notification, and in **Top-ups → Requests**.
2. Check your payment app for the payment (amount, method and tag are shown on the request).
3. Load the game to the **game username** shown.
4. Tap **Loaded** — or **Payment not received** if you can't find the payment. Add a note if useful.
   The customer is told in their chat. Each request can only be marked once.

### Chats and tickets
- **Customer Inbox**: reply to customers. They see your replies as "John Rambo Support", never your name.
- **Support Tickets**: reply to tickets assigned to you and move them along (In progress → Resolved).
  You can read other tickets but only the assigned employee or the Boss can reply.
- **JR Family**: chat with the Boss and the other employees.
- **Staff Broadcast Channel** (link on **My Dashboard**): read the Boss's announcements and acknowledge them.
- **Invite Customer**: you can invite customers (not employees) and approve new customers.

## For Customers

### Top up a game
1. Tap **Games** (on Home, or the **Games** tab).
2. Pick your game.
3. Enter your **username in that game** (remembered next time) and the **amount**.
4. Send the payment to one of the payment tags shown (tap the copy icon to copy it), then tap the method you
   used and **Send request**.
5. Follow it on the **Games** page: **Waiting** → **Loaded**. You also get a chat message.

### Other things you can do
- **Chat** with John Rambo Support any time — including to discuss a price.
- **Raise ticket** for a tracked problem (for example points not delivered). You can name an employee if you
  want to make a complaint; that opens a private room with you, that employee and the Boss.
- **My profile**: change your name, phone or password.

---

## If something goes wrong
- Server errors are emailed automatically to the alert address set at launch.
- A customer who cannot sign in: check they are **approved** and not **suspended** (Customers page).
- A customer who never received their code: invite emails come from the address set up in Resend; ask them
  to check spam, then re-send the invite.
